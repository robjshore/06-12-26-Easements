# Galleria Schedule 'A' — Easement Asymmetry & Reciprocity Analysis

> Companion to `EASEMENTS.md`. Compares easements of the **same purpose** granted for the benefit of **different parties**, and identifies **reciprocal** grants. Generated by `build_asymmetry.py` from the same dataset (do not hand-edit).

**Scope of comparison:** the three project parcels — `PK` Parking, `CM` Commercial, `B13` Blocks 1 & 3 — in their two lanes relative to the Residential Condo (`RC`): *RC grants* (Reserving/Subject To) and *RC receives* (Together With). Third-party grants (City / Rogers / Enbridge) are excluded from the symmetry analysis.

Markers: `*` = REQUIRED?-flagged · `°` = temporary.
