Galleria Schedule ‘A’ — Easement Asymmetry & Reciprocity

The same kind of right is often granted to more than one neighbour, and sometimes in both directions. This tool shows where those grants don’t match — a difference that may be deliberate, or may be an oversight worth checking.

How to read this

  1. RC→X means the Residential Condo is burdened (gives up the right) in favour of parcel X. X→RC is the reverse. The parcels are RC Residential Condo, PK Parking, CM Commercial, B13 Blocks 1 & 3.
  2. A right that appears on both RC→X and X→RC is reciprocal (granted both ways). One that appears on only one side runs one direction only — an asymmetry.
  3. Even when a right is granted to several parties, the terms can differ (scope, levels, duration, conditions). Click a purpose to see a side-by-side comparison with the differences highlighted.
The headline cards below group every difference we found into four kinds: coverage gaps (granted to some but not others), content (same right, different terms), reciprocity, and burden-vs-benefit (the Condo’s terms differ depending on whether it’s giving or receiving).
